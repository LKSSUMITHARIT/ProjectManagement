# Executive Documentation

> **Purpose:**  
> This section provides the executive overview of the **Project Management Platform**. It explains the product vision, business objectives, scope, roadmap, and guiding principles without diving into implementation details.

---

# Overview

The **Project Management Platform** is an AI-first, enterprise-grade Production and Project Management system designed specifically for Game Art, Animation, VFX, Design, and Digital Content Production studios.

Unlike traditional project management tools that focus primarily on issue tracking or task management, this platform is designed around the complete production lifecycle—from client onboarding to project delivery, review management, resource allocation, finance, and production analytics.

The platform is being architected as a configurable, workflow-driven system capable of supporting multiple industries while maintaining a strong focus on creative production pipelines.

---

# Executive Summary

The objective of this product is to provide a unified platform capable of managing the complete production lifecycle.

The system will centralize:

- Client Management
- Project Planning
- Batch Management
- Asset Management
- Production Tracking
- Task Workflow Management
- Review & Approval Process
- Resource Allocation
- Team Collaboration
- Communication
- Audit & Activity Tracking
- Finance Integration
- Production Analytics
- AI Assisted Operations

The long-term vision is to evolve the platform into an intelligent Production Operating System capable of automating planning, tracking, reporting, and decision-making through AI.

# Why This Product?

Most studios today use multiple disconnected tools.

Typical workflow:

```
CRM

↓

Excel

↓

Jira

↓

ShotGrid/Ftrack

↓

Slack

↓

Email

↓

Accounting Software

↓

Reports
```

This creates problems such as:

- Duplicate data entry
- Scattered communication
- Poor production visibility
- Manual resource planning
- Difficult review management
- Lack of audit trail
- Inefficient reporting
- Limited automation

This platform aims to unify these capabilities into a single configurable system.
