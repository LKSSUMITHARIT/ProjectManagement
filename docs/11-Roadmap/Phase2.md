# Phase 2 – Enterprise Foundation

**Document ID:** ROADMAP-P2

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Planned

**Target Release:** Version 2.0

**Estimated Duration:** 5–6 Months

**Prerequisite:** Successful completion of Phase 1 (MVP)

---

# Purpose

Phase 2 transforms the MVP into a fully featured **Enterprise Project & Asset Management Platform** capable of supporting medium and large organizations with advanced workflow automation, financial management, enterprise integrations, collaboration, and enhanced reporting.

The primary objective is to make the platform production-ready for enterprise customers while preparing the foundation for AI-driven capabilities in Phase 3.

---

# Objectives

At the completion of Phase 2, the platform shall:

- Support enterprise-scale organizations
- Provide advanced workflow automation
- Introduce financial management
- Enable external integrations
- Improve collaboration
- Support advanced reporting
- Introduce approval hierarchies
- Enhance security and auditing
- Improve scalability and performance
