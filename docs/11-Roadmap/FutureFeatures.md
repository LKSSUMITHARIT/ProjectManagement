# Purpose

This document captures potential enhancements and future capabilities that are **not currently planned for any specific release** but align with the long-term vision of the AI Project & Asset Management Platform and AI Software Factory.

These features are intentionally separated from the phased roadmap because they may depend on emerging technologies, customer demand, market trends, or future business priorities.

# Prioritization Framework

Future features should be evaluated using:

| Criteria | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Customer Value | Business impact for customers |
| Strategic Alignment | Alignment with product vision |
| Technical Feasibility | Complexity and implementation risk |
| Market Demand | Customer and industry demand |
| Revenue Potential | Ability to drive growth |
| AI Enablement | Contribution to AI-first strategy |

# Governance

Future features are not commitments.

They should be:

- Regularly reviewed
- Prioritized based on business value
- Validated with customers
- Assessed for technical feasibility
- Approved before entering the official roadmap

---

# Vision

The long-term objective is to evolve the platform into a comprehensive **Enterprise Operating System (Enterprise OS)** powered by collaborative AI agents, capable of managing projects, assets, software delivery, enterprise operations, and intelligent decision-making across organizations while remaining extensible, secure, and adaptable to future technologies.
