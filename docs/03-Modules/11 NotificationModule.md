**Document ID:** MOD-011

**Module:** Notification Module

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

**Owner:** Platform Services Team

---

# Purpose

The Notification Module is the centralized event-driven communication engine responsible for delivering timely, reliable, and configurable notifications across the entire AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

Every significant system event—whether generated by a user, workflow, AI agent, scheduler, or external integration—is processed through this module before being delivered to the intended recipients.

The module ensures users receive the right information through the right channel at the right time while minimizing notification fatigue.

---

# Objectives

The Notification Module shall:

- Centralize all platform notifications.
- Support multiple delivery channels.
- Deliver notifications in real time.
- Support scheduled notifications.
- Support reminder and escalation notifications.
- Enable user notification preferences.
- Integrate with workflows and AI.
- Support external messaging services.
- Maintain delivery history.
- Provide notification analytics.

# Business Rules

- Critical notifications cannot be muted.
- Failed notifications are retried automatically.
- Notification history is retained according to retention policy.
- Users control personal notification preferences.
- System administrators may override critical security notifications.
- Notifications generated by workflows are immutable.
- Delivery failures are logged.
