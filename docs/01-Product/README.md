# Product Overview

The Project Management Platform is an **enterprise-grade Production Management System** designed for organizations that execute structured production workflows.

Unlike generic project management tools, this platform models the complete production lifecycle, including:

- Client Management
- Project Management
- Batch Management
- Resource Allocation
- Asset Management
- Task & Subtask Management
- Workflow Automation
- Review Management
- Deliverable Tracking
- Financial Tracking
- Communication
- Reporting
- AI-Assisted Operations (Future)

The platform is designed to be configurable and adaptable across multiple industries while maintaining a production-first architecture.

---

# Product Philosophy

The platform is built around several core ideas:

- Production-first rather than generic task management.
- Workflow-driven business processes.
- Configuration over customization.
- Modular and extensible architecture.
- Enterprise scalability and security.
- Complete traceability through audit logging.
- AI-ready by design.
- API-first integration strategy.

These principles ensure the platform can evolve without compromising consistency or maintainability.
