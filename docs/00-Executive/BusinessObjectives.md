# Business Objectives

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the strategic business objectives of the Project Management Platform. These objectives describe the measurable business outcomes the platform aims to achieve for organizations rather than specific software features.

---

# Introduction

The primary objective of the Project Management Platform is to improve the operational efficiency of production-driven organizations by providing a unified, configurable, and intelligent management system.

The platform is intended to become the operational backbone of an organization by centralizing project execution, production tracking, collaboration, financial visibility, and decision support.

---

# Strategic Business Objectives

## BO-01 — Centralize Business Operations

Create a single platform that manages the complete lifecycle of projects from client onboarding to final delivery.

### Expected Outcomes

- Eliminate disconnected tools
- Reduce duplicate data
- Establish a single source of truth
- Improve operational consistency

---

## BO-02 — Standardize Production Processes

Provide configurable workflows that standardize production across projects while allowing flexibility for different business requirements.

### Expected Outcomes

- Consistent execution
- Reduced operational variations
- Faster onboarding
- Improved quality

---

## BO-03 — Increase Production Visibility

Provide real-time visibility into production activities at every organizational level.

Management should be able to monitor:

- Projects
- Batches
- Assets
- Tasks
- Reviews
- Deliverables
- Resources

### Expected Outcomes

- Better decision making
- Early risk identification
- Improved planning
- Accurate reporting

---

## BO-04 — Improve Resource Utilization

Optimize allocation of production resources across multiple projects and batches.

The platform should help organizations:

- Balance workloads
- Prevent over-allocation
- Reduce idle capacity
- Improve planning accuracy

### Expected Outcomes

- Higher utilization
- Reduced scheduling conflicts
- Better forecasting

---

## BO-05 — Streamline Review & Approval Processes

Implement structured review workflows that improve collaboration between Artists, Leads, Final Reviewers, QC Teams, and Clients.

### Expected Outcomes

- Reduced review turnaround time
- Improved feedback tracking
- Reduced communication gaps
- Better production quality

---

## BO-06 — Improve Project Predictability

Provide accurate visibility into project health and delivery progress.

The platform should enable organizations to answer:

- Are projects on schedule?
- What work is delayed?
- Where are bottlenecks?
- Which projects are at risk?

### Expected Outcomes

- Better planning
- Improved delivery confidence
- Reduced project risk

---

## BO-07 — Enhance Financial Visibility

Integrate production progress with project financial information.

Business users should be able to monitor:

- Project Billing
- Invoice Status
- Outstanding Payments
- Client Billing History

### Expected Outcomes

- Better cash flow visibility
- Improved billing accuracy
- Faster payment tracking

---

## BO-08 — Improve Collaboration

Centralize communication within the context of production work.

Discussions should remain linked to:

- Projects
- Batches
- Assets
- Tasks

### Expected Outcomes

- Reduced email dependency
- Better knowledge retention
- Faster collaboration

---

## BO-09 — Ensure Complete Traceability

Maintain a permanent history of operational activities.

Examples include:

- Workflow transitions
- Task updates
- Review history
- Resource allocation
- Financial changes
- User actions

### Expected Outcomes

- Improved governance
- Better compliance
- Easier auditing
- Complete accountability

---

## BO-10 — Enable Data-Driven Decisions

Provide operational dashboards and analytics based on real-time production data.

Examples include:

- Production progress
- Resource utilization
- Review efficiency
- Financial performance
- Delivery trends

### Expected Outcomes

- Better strategic planning
- Improved operational decisions
- Executive visibility

---

## BO-11 — Build an AI-Ready Platform

Design the platform so Artificial Intelligence can become an active participant in production management.

Future AI capabilities include:

- Resource recommendations
- Schedule optimization
- Risk prediction
- Intelligent reporting
- Review assistance
- Delivery forecasting

### Expected Outcomes

- Increased productivity
- Reduced manual planning
- Better forecasting
- Continuous optimization

---

## BO-12 — Support Enterprise Growth

Design the platform to scale with organizational growth.

The platform should support:

- Multiple clients
- Multiple projects
- Multiple business units
- Large production teams
- High transaction volumes

### Expected Outcomes

- Long-term scalability
- Reduced operational complexity
- Enterprise adoption

---

# Business Success Criteria

The platform will be considered successful if it achieves the following business outcomes.

| Objective | Success Indicator |
|------------|------------------|
| Centralized Operations | Single platform used across departments |
| Standardized Workflows | Reduced process variations |
| Improved Visibility | Real-time operational dashboards |
| Better Resource Planning | Increased utilization and reduced conflicts |
| Faster Reviews | Reduced review turnaround time |
| Financial Visibility | Accurate project billing and payment tracking |
| Improved Collaboration | Reduced external communication dependency |
| Complete Traceability | Full audit history for operational events |
| Data-Driven Management | Decisions supported by live production data |
| AI Enablement | AI-assisted planning and operational insights |
