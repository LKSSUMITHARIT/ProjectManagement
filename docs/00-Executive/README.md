# Executive Documentation

> **Purpose**
>
> The Executive Documentation provides a high-level understanding of the Project Management Platform. It defines the product vision, business objectives, strategic direction, scope, and success criteria. This section is intended for stakeholders, product owners, architects, investors, business analysts, and engineering leadership.

---

# Overview

The **Project Management Platform** is an AI-first, enterprise-grade Production & Project Management system designed to manage the complete lifecycle of production-driven projects.

The platform combines project planning, production execution, workflow management, resource allocation, review management, communication, finance, reporting, and artificial intelligence into a unified solution.

Although the initial focus is Game Art, Animation, VFX, and Digital Production, the platform is architected to support any workflow-driven organization through configurable business processes.

# Documentation Principles

The documentation repository follows several principles:

- **Single Source of Truth** — Every topic is documented once and referenced elsewhere.
- **Modular Documentation** — Each document has a single responsibility.
- **Business First** — Business concepts are defined before technical implementation.
- **Technology Agnostic** — Executive documentation focuses on product strategy rather than implementation.
- **Living Documentation** — Documentation evolves alongside the product.
- **AI-Friendly Structure** — Documents are organized to support both human readers and AI development agents.
