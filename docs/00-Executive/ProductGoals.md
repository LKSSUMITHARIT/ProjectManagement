# Product Goals

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the strategic and functional goals of the Project Management Platform. While the **Vision** describes *why* the product exists and the **Business Objectives** describe *what business outcomes* it should achieve, this document defines *what the product itself must become* to fulfill that vision.

---

# Introduction

The Project Management Platform is designed to become a comprehensive Production Management System capable of supporting the complete lifecycle of production-driven organizations.

The primary goal is not simply to manage projects, but to create a configurable platform that manages **Planning, Execution, Review, Delivery, Finance, Collaboration, Reporting, and Artificial Intelligence** within a single ecosystem.

---

# Primary Product Goals

## PG-01 — Build a Unified Production Platform

Provide a single platform capable of managing the complete production lifecycle.

The platform shall eliminate the need for multiple disconnected systems by integrating:

- Client Management
- Project Management
- Batch Management
- Asset Management
- Task Management
- Workflow Management
- Resource Management
- Review Management
- Finance
- Reporting
- Communication

---

## PG-02 — Model Real Production Workflows

Unlike generic task management systems, the platform shall model real-world production structures.

The platform shall support:

- Clients
- Projects
- Batches
- Assets
- Tasks
- Subtasks
- Deliverables
- Reviews
- Review Rounds

---

## PG-03 — Provide a Configurable Workflow Engine

Every business process shall execute through configurable workflows.

The workflow engine shall support:

- Multiple Workflows
- Multiple Processes
- Multiple States
- Configurable Transitions
- Business Rules
- Permissions
- Notifications

No workflow should require source code modification.

---

## PG-04 — Support End-to-End Production Tracking

Every production activity shall be traceable.

The system shall track:

- Task Progress
- Review Progress
- Workflow Position
- Resource Allocation
- Deliverables
- Production Status
- Financial Status

---

## PG-05 — Simplify Resource Planning

Provide a centralized resource allocation system.

The platform shall support:

- Resource Requests
- Approval Workflow
- Percentage Allocation
- Capacity Planning
- Utilization Tracking
- Multi-Batch Allocation

---

## PG-06 — Improve Review Management

Provide structured review workflows.

Supported review types include:

- Work-In-Progress Review
- Final Review
- Quality Control
- Client Review

The review system shall support:

- Multiple Review Rounds
- Feedback
- Minor Fixes
- Major Fixes
- Approval History

---

## PG-07 — Centralize Communication

Provide contextual communication directly within the platform.

Communication shall exist at:

- Project Level
- Batch Level
- Asset Level
- Task Level

The goal is to eliminate scattered communication across multiple tools.

---

## PG-08 — Maintain Complete Auditability

Every significant action performed within the platform shall be recorded.

Examples include:

- Workflow Changes
- Assignments
- Status Changes
- Reviews
- Financial Updates
- User Actions
- Configuration Changes

The platform shall maintain a complete activity timeline.

---

## PG-09 — Integrate Production and Finance

Production and financial information should remain connected.

The platform shall support:

- Project Billing
- Invoice Generation
- Payment Tracking
- Outstanding Management

Future versions may include ERP integration.

---

## PG-10 — Support Source Control Integration

Production files shall remain in external source control systems.

The platform shall manage only metadata, including:

- Repository
- File Path
- Revision
- Changeset
- Deliverable Information

This ensures complete traceability without duplicating production assets.

---

## PG-11 — Deliver Actionable Insights

Provide dashboards and reports for operational decision-making.

Examples include:

- Project Dashboard
- Batch Dashboard
- Production Dashboard
- Resource Dashboard
- Financial Dashboard
- Executive Dashboard

---

## PG-12 — Become AI Ready

The platform shall be architected to support Artificial Intelligence as a core capability.

Future AI features include:

- Intelligent Scheduling
- Risk Prediction
- AI Resource Planner
- AI Project Manager
- AI Review Assistant
- AI Analytics
- AI Recommendations

---

# Non-Functional Product Goals

The platform should also achieve the following technical goals.

## Scalability

Support organizations of varying sizes, from small studios to enterprise deployments.

---

## Performance

Provide responsive user experience even with large numbers of:

- Projects
- Assets
- Tasks
- Reviews
- Activity Logs

---

## Extensibility

Allow future capabilities through modular architecture without significant redesign.

---

## Configurability

Business administrators should configure the platform through settings rather than software changes.

---

## Security

Protect business data through enterprise-grade authentication, authorization, auditing, and secure data handling.

---

## Reliability

Provide a stable and dependable platform suitable for production-critical operations.

---

# Long-Term Product Goals

The long-term evolution of the platform includes:

- AI Production Operating System
- Intelligent Workflow Automation
- Predictive Production Analytics
- Digital Asset Intelligence
- Automated Capacity Planning
- Cross-Project Optimization
- Client Self-Service Portal
- Marketplace for Extensions
- Mobile Applications
- Open Integration Platform

---

# Product Success Indicators

The product goals will be considered achieved when the platform:

- Manages the complete production lifecycle.
- Supports configurable workflows without code changes.
- Provides complete production visibility.
- Enables efficient collaboration.
- Simplifies resource planning.
- Reduces review turnaround time.
- Maintains complete audit history.
- Integrates operational and financial data.
- Supports AI-assisted decision-making.
- Scales for enterprise production environments.
