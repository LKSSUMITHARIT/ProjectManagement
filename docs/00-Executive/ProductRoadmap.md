# Product Roadmap

> **Purpose**
>
> This document defines the strategic roadmap for the Project Management Platform. It outlines the planned evolution of the product across multiple phases, from the initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) to a fully AI-driven Production Operating System.
>
> The roadmap is **vision-oriented rather than time-bound**. Actual release schedules may vary based on business priorities, customer feedback, and technical considerations.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

The platform will be developed incrementally.

Each phase must:

- Deliver usable business value.
- Be production ready.
- Build upon previous capabilities.
- Maintain backward compatibility.
- Preserve modular architecture.
- Prepare the platform for future AI integration.

# Roadmap Principles

The roadmap will follow these guiding principles:

- Business Value First
- Customer Feedback Driven
- Modular Delivery
- Backward Compatibility
- Enterprise Scalability
- AI-Ready Architecture
- Security by Default
- Performance at Scale

---

# Release Governance

A feature may move between phases based on:

- Customer demand
- Business priority
- Technical dependencies
- Development effort
- Strategic alignment
- Regulatory or compliance requirements

The roadmap should remain flexible while preserving the long-term product vision.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The ultimate objective is to evolve from a traditional project management solution into an **AI-powered Production Operating System** capable of managing, optimizing, and continuously improving production workflows across multiple industries.

The platform should become the central operational hub where planning, execution, collaboration, finance, analytics, and AI-driven insights work together to enable efficient and predictable project delivery.
