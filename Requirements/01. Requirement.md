# 1. Purpose

This document serves as the master Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for the AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

It provides the overall vision, scope, objectives, architecture overview, business context, and references to all detailed requirement specifications.

This document is intended to act as the single entry point for understanding the product while delegating implementation-level details to specialized requirement documents.

---

# 2. Product Overview

The AI Project & Asset Management Platform is an enterprise-grade SaaS application designed to manage the complete lifecycle of projects, digital assets, production workflows, reviews, resource allocation, financial operations, reporting, and AI-assisted decision making.

Unlike traditional project management software, the platform combines:

- Project Management
- Asset Management
- Workflow Automation
- Resource Planning
- Financial Tracking
- Review Management
- AI Assistance
- Enterprise Reporting

into one unified ecosystem.

---

# 3. Vision Statement

To build an AI-first enterprise platform that manages projects, people, workflows, digital assets, and business operations through configurable workflows, intelligent automation, and actionable analytics.

---

# 4. Problem Statement

Organizations frequently use multiple disconnected tools to manage:

- Projects
- Tasks
- Assets
- Reviews
- Resource Allocation
- Financial Tracking
- Communication
- Reporting

This results in:

- Duplicate data
- Poor visibility
- Manual coordination
- Inefficient workflows
- Limited automation
- High operational costs

The platform aims to consolidate these capabilities into a single extensible system.

# 8. Product Architecture Overview

The system follows a modular architecture.

```text
Presentation Layer

↓

Application Layer

↓

Business Modules

↓

Workflow Engine

↓

Data Access Layer

↓

Database

↓

AI Services

↓

External Integrations
```

The architecture is service-oriented and designed for future modular deployment.

# 26. Assumptions

The following assumptions apply:

- Users have authenticated access.
- The platform supports multi-tenancy.
- Internet connectivity is available for SaaS deployments.
- External integrations expose supported APIs.
- AI services may operate locally or through external providers.
- Business workflows are configurable without code changes.

---

# 27. Constraints

- The platform must support modular deployment.
- All business logic must be workflow-driven where applicable.
- Security and auditing are mandatory.
- APIs must follow REST standards.
- All modules must support role-based authorization.
