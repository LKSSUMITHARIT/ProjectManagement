# 1. Purpose

This document defines the reporting and analytics requirements for the AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

The reporting subsystem shall provide operational, tactical, strategic, and executive reporting capabilities with real-time dashboards, historical analytics, AI-driven insights, and customizable reporting tools.

---

# 2. Objectives

The reporting platform shall enable users to:

- Monitor business performance
- Track project progress
- Measure productivity
- Analyze financial performance
- Monitor workflow efficiency
- Identify production bottlenecks
- Forecast trends
- Export business reports
- Schedule automated reports
- Generate AI-powered insights

---
