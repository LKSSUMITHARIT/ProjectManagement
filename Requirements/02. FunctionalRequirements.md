# 1. Purpose

This document defines all functional requirements for the AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

Functional requirements describe what the system shall do from a business and user perspective.

These requirements are implementation-independent and serve as the baseline for architecture, development, testing, and acceptance.

# 2. Scope

- Asset Management
