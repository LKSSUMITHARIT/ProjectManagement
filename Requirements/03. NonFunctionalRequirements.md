# 1. Purpose

This document defines the non-functional requirements (NFRs) for the AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

Non-functional requirements define **how the system shall perform** rather than **what the system shall do**.

These requirements establish the quality attributes, operational characteristics, constraints, and technical standards that govern the platform.

# 4. Performance Requirements

## NFR-PER-001

The system shall support at least **5,000 concurrent authenticated users**.

Priority: High

---

## NFR-PER-002

95% of page requests shall complete within **2 seconds**.

---

## NFR-PER-003

95% of API requests shall respond within **500 milliseconds** under normal operating conditions.

---

## NFR-PER-004

Dashboard loading shall complete within **3 seconds**.

---

## NFR-PER-005

Search results shall be displayed within **2 seconds**.

---

## NFR-PER-006

Large report generation shall complete within **60 seconds**.

---

## NFR-PER-007

Bulk operations shall execute asynchronously when processing exceeds configurable thresholds.

---

## NFR-PER-008

Long-running background jobs shall not block user interactions.

---

# 22. Acceptance Criteria

A non-functional requirement shall be considered satisfied when:

- It is measurable.
- It can be verified through testing, inspection, or monitoring.
- It is documented in deployment or operational procedures where applicable.
- It does not conflict with approved functional requirements.
