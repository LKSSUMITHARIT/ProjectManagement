# 1. Purpose

This document defines all business rules governing the AI Project & Asset Management Platform.

Business Rules represent policies, constraints, validations, calculations, approvals, and decision logic that the system shall enforce regardless of implementation technology.

Business Rules are independent of:

- UI
- API
- Database
- Programming Language
- Deployment Model

---

# 5. General Business Rules

---

## BR-GEN-001

### Organization Isolation

Every record belongs to exactly one Organization.

Cross-organization data access is prohibited unless explicitly authorized.

Priority

Critical

---

## BR-GEN-002

Soft Delete

Business records shall be soft deleted unless permanent deletion is explicitly permitted.

---

## BR-GEN-003

Audit Trail

Every business transaction shall create an immutable audit record.

---

## BR-GEN-004

Activity History

Every significant business action shall appear in the activity history.

---

## BR-GEN-005

Created Date

Every entity shall record:

- Created By
- Created Date
- Modified By
- Modified Date

---

## BR-GEN-006

Unique Identifier

Every business entity shall have a globally unique identifier.

---

## BR-GEN-007

Workflow Enforcement

If an entity supports workflows, status changes shall only occur through valid workflow transitions.

---

## BR-GEN-008

Reference Integrity

Referenced entities shall exist before relationships are created.

---

## BR-GEN-009

Business Validation Before Save

Validation shall occur before data persistence.

---

## BR-GEN-010

Configuration Overrides Code

Business behavior should be configurable through metadata wherever practical.

---

# 21. Rule Conflict Resolution

If multiple Business Rules conflict:

1. Security Rules override all others.
2. Regulatory and Compliance Rules take precedence.
3. Organization-level rules override Project-level rules.
4. Workflow Rules override manual operations.
5. More specific rules override general rules.
6. Higher-priority rules override lower-priority rules.
